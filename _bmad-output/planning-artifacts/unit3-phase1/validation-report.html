Decision-readiness

Broken

The authorization target is indeterminate.

  • Critical: Phase 1 promises the intelligence core and manual circles, but current journeys and FRs also mandate Phase 2/3 sensing, integration, entry, analytics, certificate, RBAC, and parity capabilities.
  • High: propagation-depth, cycle, precedence, override, and public-health policy decisions are not exposed.
  • High: mandatory gate enforcement, voluntary check-in, biometrics, deletion, and anonymity trade-offs have no owner or open-question record.

Substance over theater

Thin
  • FR-24–FR-38 introduce platform capabilities without a supporting journey, outcome, or Phase 1 rationale.
  • The user list lacks constraints and conflicts that would drive product decisions.

Strategic coherence

Thin
  • SC-1, FR-11, and NFR-1 use incompatible or incomplete timing clocks.
  • SC-4 can reward over-fencing and has no counter-metric for missed contacts or unnecessary fence-hours.
  • SC-3 measures a smart detection workflow deferred beyond Phase 1.

Done-ness clarity

Broken
  • Critical: the edge predicate and recursive fence/release algorithm lack one implementable meaning.
  • Critical: retained identity mapping and biometrics contradict the claimed non-reversible anonymity.
  • Many FRs lack actor, precondition, error/empty state, permissions, and observable acceptance outcome.
  • FR-28 prescribes Kubernetes PersistentVolume while omitting secure upload behavior.

Scope honesty

Broken
  • The PRD has no explicit MVP non-goals despite many deferred capabilities.
  • WiFi/BLE accuracy, infrastructure availability, gate hardware, integrations, scale, and staffing are unmarked assumptions.
  • External authority, consent, accessibility, biometrics, and certificate provenance have no accountable open-item mechanism.

Downstream usability

Broken
  • Status, circle, identity, anonymity, and fence terms drift without a glossary.
  • No phase → outcome → journey → requirement traceability exists.
  • UJ-2, UJ-4, and UJ-5 lack a sufficiently specific role-based protagonist.

Shape fit

Broken
  • Critical: FERPA and deletion are asserted rather than traced to data classes, purposes, roles, retention, evidence, and tests.
  • High-impact exception journeys—consent refusal, appeal, offline/degraded use, conflicting evidence, accessibility, abuse, and surge—are absent.
  • Implementation choices are fixed before the governing policies are resolved.
Decision gate: Do not approve Phase 1 implementation until all four critical blockers and the Phase 1 scope/traceability defects are resolved. IDs SC-1–SC-5, UJ-1–UJ-6, DR-1–DR-6, FR-1–FR-38, and NFR-1–NFR-8 are contiguous; no assumptions index, open-questions section, glossary, acceptance-criteria set, or traceability matrix exists.
